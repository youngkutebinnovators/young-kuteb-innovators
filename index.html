Young Kuteb Innovators (YKI) is an independent, youth-led initiative in development, built to help young people learn practical digital skills, connect with mentors, turn ideas into solutions, and create opportunities for their communities.

Who we are

Local roots. Wider possibilities.

YKI aims to connect young people to the knowledge, tools, relationships, and opportunities they need to create a better future. We are beginning with a practical pilot and building through listening, collaboration, and measurable action.

Our vision

A generation empowered to transform communities

To raise a generation of empowered Kuteb and other participating young people who use innovation, technology, entrepreneurship, and creativity to transform their communities and contribute to the world.

Our mission

Skills, mentorship and ideas that become solutions

To equip young people with practical digital skills, mentorship, entrepreneurial opportunities, and an inclusive innovation ecosystem that transforms local ideas into sustainable solutions while preserving and celebrating Kuteb cultural heritage.

Culture with care

Innovation that honours our heritage.

Kuteb identity is an important inspiration for YKI. We want cultural preservation to be community-led, respectful and useful to future generations—not something collected or published without permission.

Our heritage principles

We will listen, consult, give credit, protect sensitive knowledge, and make room for different accounts and perspectives.

  • Seek informed consent before recording or publishing.
  • Respect knowledge that should remain private or restricted.
  • Agree on attribution, permissions and reuse.
  • Work with elders, artists, historians, linguists and youth.
  • Use culture to inspire creativity, learning and community benefit.
Start small. Learn fast.

A practical first 90 days.

We will focus on proof of value before scaling. The first phase is about listening, delivering a small pilot, and sharing results honestly.

DAYS 01–30

Establish the foundation

Consult young people and community stakeholders, recruit founding volunteers, identify needs, and prepare simple operating and safeguarding guidelines.

DAYS 31–60

Run the pilot

Invite a small first cohort, deliver an introductory digital skills session, host a problem-solving activity, and connect participants with available mentors.

DAYS 61–90

Show what we learn

Support practical projects, host a virtual showcase, publish a short report, and use the evidence to improve the next phase and approach partners.
